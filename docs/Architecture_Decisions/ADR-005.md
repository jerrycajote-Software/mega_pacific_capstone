# ADR-005: Decouple Customer Pre-Orders from Finished Inventory and Track Raw Steel Coils

## Context
Industrial metal roofing manufacturing operates as made-to-order (roll-formed cut to length) rather than static shelf stock. Customers specify custom cut lengths (0.5m to 21.0m) and color/gauge combinations. Restricting customer orders to a static finished goods stock count resulted in false out-of-stock errors and prevented realistic orders. Furthermore, physical production consumes master steel coils in linear meters rather than discrete roofing sheets.

## Decision
1. **Decouple Customer Orders from Finished Goods Stock**: Finished roofing profiles have `isPreOrder: true` and are never gated or deducted from a static sheet counter. Order line item subtotal pricing dynamically evaluates as:
   `Subtotal = Unit Price per Meter × Custom Length (meters) × Quantity (sheets)`.
2. **Shift Inventory Management to Raw Steel Coils (`CoilInventory`)**: Internal business operations (Logistics and Owner) track 56 raw steel coils in linear meters across 14 official Mega Pacific colors and 4 gauges (0.35mm, 0.40mm, 0.50mm, 0.60mm).
3. **Manual Material Usage Deduction by Logistics**: Logistics handlers record exact meters cut per job/order or scrap loss with an audit log (`CoilInventoryLog`).
4. **Sales Pre-Order Product Management**: Sales representatives manage customizable roofing catalog items matching official price lists with profile dimensions (effective width, nominal width, rib height), allowable colors, and thickness pricing variants.

## Rationale
- Accurately mirrors real-world roll-forming operations where sheets do not exist until ordered and cut from master raw coils.
- Prevents artificial cart and checkout blocking for high-volume custom sheet orders.
- Provides genuine material accountability and replenishment visibility for warehouse and executive management.

## Status
**Accepted**
